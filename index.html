Every creative below is the file an ad server would receive: one HTML document, no external requests at runtime, system fonts only. Nothing here is a screenshot or a video — you are looking at the running creative.

Mobile gaming

Neon Star Shooter — pick a planet

970 × 250

Six planets, twenty levels each, twenty bosses. The banner runs the game's own planet selector: tap a world and the scene, the name and the accent colour change. Six full-bleed game scenes are WebP-compressed and embedded in the file — 143 KB against the IAB 150 KB initial-load limit.

Tap a planet along the bottom.

Playable ad — twenty seconds, then the store

Fullscreen

A hyper-casual runner that plays inside the ad. Tap to jump. Built four times, one per network: mraid.open(), FbPlayableAd.onCTAClick(), ExitApi.exit(), window.openAppStore(). This is the MRAID build. Everything is drawn in code — no image, font or model file ships in the bundle.

Tap anywhere to jump.

Real estate

Where the sun lands, hour by hour

970 × 250

The question a listing cannot answer is "does the balcony get afternoon sun?". Drag the sun around the floor plan and each room lights by the real bearing of its windows: the south-facing living room all day, the east bedroom until midday, the north-facing kitchen never — the banner says so rather than faking a glow. Drag the sun along the dotted orbit.

Design to HTML5
